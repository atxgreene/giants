# LORE BIBLE — The Watchers: Fall of the Giants

> Working rule for all writers: **the game is disclosure fiction, not doctrine.**
> Every claim in the game world carries a source tier, and the player can always
> see which shelf a "fact" came from. We never flatten the layers.

---

## 1. The Source-Tier Methodology (the spine of the whole project)

The in-game codex, **The Witness Archive**, tags every entry:

| Tier | Label | What belongs here | Examples in slice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Canonical Scripture | Direct biblical text and what it plainly says | Nephilim (Gen 6:4), Michael (Dan 10/12, Jude 9, Rev 12), Gabriel (Dan 8–9, Luke 1) |
| 2 | Enochic Tradition | 1 Enoch / Book of the Watchers material; Tobit for Raphael | The Watchers' oath, Azazel's weapons-teaching (1 En 8), giant spirits (1 En 15) |
| 3 | Book of Giants Fragment | Qumran fragments (4Q530 etc.) and their actual gaps | The giants' dreams, the tablet in the water, the three names |
| 4 | Scholarly Reconstruction | Live academic disputes, presented as disputes | What "Azazel" means in Leviticus 16; etymology of "Nephilim" |
| 5 | Game-Original Mythic Extrapolation | Our inventions, honestly flagged | The Appointed Witness office, Bloodlings, the Watchtower, the First Blade |
| 6 | Unknown / Hidden | The Archive refusing to say | The Scribe of Dust, the Final Revelation |

**Honesty rules:** never state Tier-4 disputes as settled. Never silently
promote a Tier-5 invention into a lower tier. Mystery entries (Tier 6) may
gesture, never assert. Where translations vary (1 Enoch 8:1), say "trans. varies."

## 2. Cosmology of the game world

- **Before the flood:** two hundred Watchers descended at Hermon and swore a
  mutual oath. They took wives; they taught — metallurgy (Azazel), sorcery,
  astrology, cosmetics. The children were the giants: half spirit, half flesh.
- **The judgment:** the earth cried out. Four were sent: Raphael to bind
  Azazel in the desert Dudael; Gabriel to set the giants against each other;
  Michael to bind Shemihazah; Uriel to warn the one righteous house. The flood
  took the giants' bodies. Their spirits remained — the game's wandering,
  ravenous "evil spirits upon the earth."
- **Now (game present, vaguely late antique / mythic):** the Watchers are
  bound but their *works* are loose: cults that re-teach the forbidden arts,
  bloodlines that thin but never quite end, spirits that pool in old
  battlefields, and lieutenants — forged things like the First Blade — that
  keep the lessons alive. Heaven's response is not an army. It is a witness.
- **Future arcs (Tier 5, planned):** the works survive into the modern day as
  occult infiltration — boardrooms instead of forges. Same lesson, new metal.

## 3. The Appointed Witness (player)

Mortal. A scribe first — someone trained to copy precisely, which is to say,
to *see* precisely. Exiled for recording something a king wanted unrecorded.
The archangels did not choose the Witness for purity ("You were not chosen
because you are pure. You were chosen because you are free"): angels and
devils both operate under constraints of office; a free human signature is
the one thing neither side can forge.

- **Corruption** is what the Watchers' works write into the Witness.
- **Revelation** is what the Witness manages to truly see and survive seeing.
- The sword is borrowed. The testimony is theirs.

## 4. The Archangel Patrons

**MICHAEL — Prince of the Host.** War as protection, never appetite. Stern,
economical, checks that you came back before he says anything else. Mechanics:
stagger, judgment damage, the Verdict. He disapproves of the Edge of Azazel
and says so exactly once.

**GABRIEL — Voice of Revelation.** A being made of message. Cryptic but never
coy without purpose; every riddle is load-bearing. Mechanics: marks, doors,
Revelation gain. Holds one undelivered announcement and is in no hurry.

**RAPHAEL — Healer of the Wound of the World.** He bound Azazel the first
time; the desert still has his handwriting in it, and he grieves walking
near it. Compassionate, slow, completely unsentimental about poison.
Mechanics: healing, corruption resistance, mercy-that-pays (seal heals).

**URIEL — Flame over the Abyss.** Severe, cosmic, faintly amused. Knows where
all the fallen stars landed. Locked until the First Forge falls — fire is not
given to people who haven't yet refused something. Mechanics: starfire burn,
ember trails, split bolts, sight within fire (slow-mo dodge).

**THE SCRIBE OF DUST.** The Archive's keeper. Human-shaped, patient,
unfileable. The oldest files are in the same handwriting. (Tier 6. The Enoch
question is *asked* by the game and *never answered* in the slice.)

## 5. The Desert of Azazel (Biome 1)

The contested wilderness of Leviticus 16 and 1 Enoch 10, rendered as red sand
over a buried prisoner. Visual bible: red dunes, black basalt walls, black
iron forges still warm, broken altars facing the wrong way, giant ribcages
as architecture, molten cracks that — mapped — radiate from a single buried
point, bronze idols with ember eyes, scapegoat cliffs, ash storms, fallen
star fragments glowing lapis-blue.

The cult here ("Ash Thralls", "Star-Sick", "Iron-Taught") is digging. The
mini-boss is what they found first. The boss is what guards what they're
digging *toward*.

## 6. Azazel's First Blade (Boss)

Not Azazel — his first artifact, made before swords had names: a forge that
walks, beautiful and wrong. Radiant corrupted wings (gold shading to rust),
stacked iron halos, orbiting sword fragments. It does not hate the Witness;
it *evangelizes*. Its temptation is sincere — the Edge of Azazel genuinely
works forever, which is the whole theology of forbidden knowledge in one
mechanic. Its dying line ("The forge does not go out. It only changes hands")
is the thesis of the sequel arc.

## 7. Tone & writing style guide

- Dialogue: short declaratives. Angels do not banter; they *land*.
- Scripture-adjacent cadence without pastiche; never quote canon mockingly.
- Horror is moral, not gory: the worst thing in the desert is a lesson.
- Humor budget: the Scribe, and one line of Uriel per scene, maximum.
- Never name the Witness. The save file is their name.
